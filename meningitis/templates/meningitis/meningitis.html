    Add or update cases from a workbook

    Upload the regional meningitis line list (.xlsx: its Outbreak linelist and Outbreak linelist-other region sheets are read) or a line list downloaded from this page and filled in. Cases are matched by EPID number. Rows with no EPID number are skipped. Blank cells never erase anything. Codes written in other ways, such as “9 unknown” or “Missing”, are read back to the list. New sub-district names are added to their district. You review every change before it is saved.

    Sub-districts and populations

    The weekly alert and epidemic thresholds of each sub-district come from its population. A sub-district with no population is checked against the small-population case counts below. Sub-districts named in an upload are added here without a population.

    Thresholds (WHO meningitis belt)

    Above the population cut-off, a sub-district is on alert when its weekly attack rate reaches the alert rate, and in epidemic when it reaches the epidemic rate. At or below the cut-off, the week's case count decides.
